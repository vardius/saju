Under the hood

How the chart is computed

Real astronomy, the classic rules, and scores worked out in the open. All of it on your device.

계산하는 방법

1The moment of birth 절기

The sun’s position decides the year and the month.

  • The sun is computed for the exact moment of birth. A truncated VSOP87 series (the standard from Meeus’s Astronomical Algorithms) with nutation, aberration and ΔT, so the solar terms land within about a minute of the published ones.
  • The year turns at Ipchun (입춘), when the sun reaches 315°, usually on 3 or 4 February. Born before it, you belong to the year before.
  • Months follow the twelve solar terms, one every 30° of the sun, not the calendar months. The month’s stem follows the year’s by the five-tigers rule.

2True solar time 진태양시

The clock is corrected before the day and the hour are read.

  • The birthplace’s longitude moves the clock four minutes for every degree away from the zone’s meridian. Seoul runs about 32 minutes behind Korean clock time.
  • The equation of time adds the sun’s seasonal wobble, up to about 16 minutes either way, from the same solar position.
  • Time zones and daylight saving come from the time-zone database on your device, historical rules included, so a 1955 birth in Seoul or a summer birth in Warsaw gets the offset it had. A time the clocks skipped is read with the offset from just before the change (02:30 becomes 03:30 summer time); a time they repeated is taken the first time round.
  • The day changes at solar midnight, and 23:00–00:59 is the Rat hour. An option on the birthplace question keeps the clock time as it was, for anyone who reads without the correction.

3The pillars and what sits on them 십성 · 십이운성

The classic tables, before any interpretation.

  • The day pillar comes from the sixty-day count anchored on the Julian Day Number (1 October 1949 was a 甲子 day). The hour pillar uses the two-hour branches and the five-rats rule for its stem.
  • The ten gods compare each stem, and each branch’s main hidden stem, with the day master by element and polarity.
  • Life stages (십이운성) follow the day stem around the branches: yang stems forwards from their 長生 branch, yin stems backwards. Hidden stems (지장간) are listed in the classic 여기, 중기, 정기 order, each with its ten god and its stage on the day branch.
  • Relationships between pillars: stem bonds (合) and clashes (沖); for the branches, six harmonies (六合), team-ups (三合, and 半合 only with the centre animal), clashes, punishments (刑), breaks (破), harms (害), resentments (怨嗔) and ghost gates (鬼門). Checked against two charts from a Korean 만세력 app.
  • Special traits (신살) use the usual tables: peach blossom, travelling horse and canopy from the year and day animals, nobleman, blade and scholar from the day stem, moon and heaven virtue from the month, and the void (공망) of the day pillar’s decade.

4Elements and strength 오행 · 신강

Counts first, then a weighted score.

  • The element balance counts the eight characters you can see, stems and branches alike.
  • Strength is a score out of 100: the month’s animal counts 40, the day’s animal 15, the other stems 10 each and the other animals 7.5 each. The day master’s own element counts in full and the element that feeds it at 85%. 55 and above is strong, 40 and below is gentle, in between is balanced. The elements that do you good follow from it: a strong day master likes what drains it, a gentle one what feeds it, and a balanced chart, which still leans a little to one side of 50, is helped gently from the side it leans away from. Which element a chart has none of plays no part in this.

5Timing 대운 · 세운 · 일진

Decades, the curve, and the years, months and days.

  • Decades run forwards for men born in a yang year and women born in a yin year, backwards otherwise. The age the first one starts counts the calendar days to the nearest solar term, both ends included, divides by three and rounds a remainder of two up, which gives the ages Korean 만세력 apps show. Both ends are dated on the clock the day pillar uses.
  • The luck curve scores each decade from 50: up or down by how its elements suit your day master, a little for the flavour of its ten god, and a little for how its animal gets on with your day’s animal.
  • Years, months and days are read the same way: the ten god of their stem gives the theme, and their animal is checked against your year and day animals for clashes, harmonies (a six harmony, or a team-up with the centre animal) and star years.

6Two people 궁합

The match score, in the open.

  • Partner stars: the ten god each day master is to the other. The power star is the partner star for a woman, the wealth star for a man.
  • Ties between the charts: every bond and clash between the eight pillars, weighted by importance: day pillar to day pillar ×2, year animals ×1.5, anything touching a day pillar ×1, the rest ×0.5.
  • Gifts: an element one chart has none of that the other has at least twice, and a day master whose element does the other good.
  • The score starts at 50, adds those terms, and is kept between 5 and 97, then named from Chaotic to Textbook.

7Where your details go 개인정보

Nowhere. Everything is computed on your phone or in your browser, and nothing is sent to a server: the faces and the list of 34,000 birthplaces ship with the app. A town too small for the list is found on a map, whose images load from OpenFreeMap; your details never go with them. What you enter stays on your device, and Start over in Settings removes it.

It’s for fun, and honest about being for fun.
