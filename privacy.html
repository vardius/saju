Privacy

Your details stay with you

Palja reads your chart on your own device, in the app or in your browser. There is no account, and your birth details are never sent anywhere.

개인정보

1What stays on your device

Your birth date, time and place, the names and birth details of anyone you add, and your settings. They are kept in the app’s own storage on your phone, or in your browser on the website, and nowhere else.

If you share a link to your chart, or ask someone how you match, your birth details travel inside the link, through the app you send it with, to whoever you send it to. Palja’s website never receives them: browsers keep the part of a link after the # to themselves.

Start over in Settings removes all of it. Deleting the app does too.

2What Palja never does

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no cookies. Nothing you type is sent to a server: the chart, the list of birthplaces and the fonts all ship with the app.

One thing comes from the internet, and only if you open it: the map for finding a town too small for the list. Its images load from OpenFreeMap, which sees which part of the map you look at, the way any website sees the pages you visit. Your birth details never go with them, and the time zone of the place you pick is worked out on your device.

3Buying Palja+ or a report

Purchases go through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. The store handles the payment and tells the app only what you own; your birth details are never part of a purchase. Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies cover the payment.

4The website

The website is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log visits (for example an IP address) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The site itself sets no cookies.

5Children

Palja is not meant for children under 13, and knowingly collects nothing from anyone, children included.
