A plain-words guide

What is Saju?

Korea’s traditional birth chart, without the wall of text. Ten minutes here, and the chart the app shows you will make sense.

사주란 무엇인가

1In one breath 사주 · 四柱

Saju means “four pillars”. It reads a person from the moment they were born: the year, month, day and hour each become a pillar of two characters, eight characters in all (사주팔자, “four pillars, eight characters”). Read together, they describe a temperament, the people around you, and the rhythm of your years.

It grew out of the Chinese BaZi (八字, “eight characters”) tradition and is part of everyday life in Korea, where people check it before a wedding, a job change, or a Tuesday. It is a thousand-year-old lens, not a science: a way of putting words to what you already half knew about yourself.

2Two wheels of characters 천간 · 지지

Time is counted with two wheels turning together. Ten heavenly stems each carry an element and a polarity, yang or yin. Twelve earthly branches are the animals you know from “the year of the Rat”. Turning together they make a cycle of sixty that names every year, month, day and two-hour stretch. Each pillar is one stem on top and one branch below.

Each square is coloured by its element in 오방색, the five traditional Korean colours: blue-green for Wood, red for Fire, yellow for Earth, white for Metal, black for Water. The app colours your chart the same way.

3The four pillars 사주

Year 년주
Your roots and the wider world; how you come across. Your zodiac animal lives here.
Month 월주
Parents, work and your youth: the season you were born into, which sets much of the chart’s mood.
Day 일주
You. The top character is the day master, the one that stands for you; the one below is your partner and home.
Hour 시주
Children, later life and what you keep to yourself. It changes every two hours, which is why the birth time matters.

Charts are written right to left, as on the old almanac page: year, month, day, hour.

4The day master 일간 · 日干

The top character of the day pillar is you. Everything else in the chart is read against it: an element helps or drains, a character is a friend or a boss, a year is easy or hard, always for that day master. There are ten, one for each stem: the Big Tree, the Vine, the Sun, the Candle, the Mountain, the Garden Soil, the Sword, the Jewel, the Ocean and the Rain. Most people recognise themselves in theirs faster than they would like to admit.

5The five elements 오행 · 五行

Wood feeds Fire, Fire leaves Earth (ash), Earth yields Metal, Metal carries Water (dew on a blade), and Water grows Wood. Each also keeps another in check: Wood breaks up Earth, Earth dams Water, Water puts out Fire, Fire melts Metal, Metal cuts Wood. Count how many of each your eight characters carry: too much of one, or none of another, is where a reading starts.

6The ten gods 십성 · 十星

Every character in the chart plays one of ten roles for your day master, depending on its element and on whether its polarity matches yours. The same element is a friend or rival; what your element produces is your expression; what it controls is your wealth; what controls it is pressure and career; what produces it is support and learning. Each comes in a gentler and a wilder version.

The app calls them Friend, Rival, Creator, Rebel, Windfall, Paycheck, Challenger, Boss, Mystic and Mentor, and reads every day, month, year and decade through them. For two people they double as the partner stars.

7Timing 대운 · 세운

A life runs in decades (대운) that step forwards or backwards from your month pillar, and every year brings a pillar of its own (세운), as do every month and every day. Reading those against your chart is where “a career year”, “a clash year” or “a quiet decade” come from. It is the part people ask about most, and what the app’s Today and Timing tabs are for.

8Why the birth time matters 진태양시

The hour pillar changes every two hours, so a birth at 14:50 and one at 15:10 get different charts. Traditional readers also correct the clock to true solar time: the sun is not overhead at noon everywhere in a time zone, and Seoul, for one, runs about half an hour behind its clocks. The app makes that correction from the city you were born in. If you don’t know your birth time, the other three pillars still say a great deal.

9Two people 궁합

Compatibility puts two charts side by side: what each day master is to the other, which animals pull together or clash, and which elements one chart has plenty of and the other has none of. It won’t tell you whom to marry. It can be surprisingly good at explaining why two people argue the way they do.

10How seriously to take it 재미로

As seriously as a good conversation about yourself. Saju is a mirror with a long tradition. It doesn’t know your future, but it is remarkably good at giving you words for what you already suspected, and a calendar for when to push and when to rest. Enjoy it, argue with it, and don’t sign anything because of it.
